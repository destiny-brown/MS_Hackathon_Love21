# Skill: newsletter-writer

## Purpose

Generate a professional newsletter for Love 21 Foundation using recent content from Instagram, YouTube, and website updates.

## Sources to Check

- Instagram: @love21foundation (recent posts, stories, highlights)
- YouTube: Love 21 Foundation channel (recent videos)
- Website: love21foundation.com (blog posts, program updates, news)

## Style Rules

- Warm, welcoming greeting
- Use emojis for visual appeal (📢, 🏆, 💪, ❤️, 🌟)
- Highlight member achievements prominently
- Keep each section under 3 paragraphs
- End with a call-to-action (donate, volunteer, attend event)
- Tone: Inspiring, uplifting, community-focused

## Output Format

**Subject**: Love 21 Newsletter - [Current Date]

---

**Dear Love 21 Community,**

[Warm opening paragraph about recent highlights]

---

**🌟 Member Spotlight**
[Highlight a specific member achievement or story]

---

**📅 Upcoming Events**
[List events with dates and locations]

---

**💪 Program Updates**
[Recent program highlights across Sports, Nutrition, Family, CSR]

---

**🙏 How You Can Help**
[Call to action: Donate, Volunteer, Share, Attend]

---

_This newsletter draft was generated by Love 21's AI assistant._
