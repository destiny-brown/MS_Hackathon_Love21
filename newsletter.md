## A Summer Update from Love 21

Dear friends,

Every week, our community proves something simple and powerful: when given the
chance, neurodiverse individuals don't just participate — they thrive. Through
sport, nutrition, and holistic family support, we're helping over 500 families
across Hong Kong reach their full potential. Here's a look at what we've been
building together.

---

## 💛 Recap: Beyond Limits Banquet

**An evening that lived up to its name**

Our Beyond Limits Banquet at Lippo Chiuchow Restaurant, Admiralty was a
celebration of ability, joy, and connection. Guests enjoyed a lively dance
performance and a fun magic show by our members, and co-emcee **Nathan Leung** —
a proud self-advocate with Down syndrome and a role model of independent living —
brought laughter and heart to the stage alongside our founder Jeff. Every table
sold helped fund fitness sessions, ABA therapy, and counselling for the families
we serve.

---

## 🏃 Our Programmes

**Sports — designed without limitations**
From strength training to coordination and mental-health activities, our sports
programme gives beneficiaries the greatest opportunity to reach their full
potential — while striving for excellence in every sport.

**Nutrition — extending healthy lives**
Sport alone isn't enough to significantly extend life expectancy. Since launching
our comprehensive nutrition programme in 2021, we've provided one-on-one
nutritional support plus regular cooking and food-prep lessons for families.

**Family — support that lifts everyone**
Our focus on family sets us apart. We offer parent-only specialty classes and
welcome parental participation across our sport and healthy-lifestyle classes —
and we've recently expanded into counselling support for parents.

**CSR — ability that brings out the best in people**
Corporate partners meet our community face to face, breaking misunderstanding
through shared activity. As one volunteer put it: "It was an incredible experience
and one that will stay with us for a long time." Interested? Email our Founder/CEO
at jeff@love21foundation.com.
